## Why Starbucks failed in Australia?
### Proposal
In your groups, watch the video on Starbucks’ failed market entry into Australia and think about answers to the following questions:
1. How did Starbucks enter the Australian market (what was the market entry strategy?)
2. Why did the market entry fail
3. What could have been done better?
4. What are the main lessons learned
Watch the video and take some key notes about the questions above until tomorrow’s class. We will discuss the video in the beginning of the class.
Write a short (about 2 pages) reflection and submit it into Reppu by Friday, SEP 11 (22:00).
### Answers
1. They started "all in", growing really quickly all over the country, without adapting its business to the country.
2. Australia have a very strong coffee culture, focused in specialized premium coffees, similar to italy and greece. Starbucks offered sweet sugary drinks for high prices. The fast growth took away the feeling of exclusivity, because it was avaliable everywhere.
3. Local menu adaptation or licensing; Organic growth
4. Brands and companies should respect local culture.
