# Human Resources Management for Small and Medium Enterprises

## Introduction - Section review questions
### 1. How can HRM be defined and what important activities does it cover?
    HRM can be defined as the management of the relationship between the employees and the company, providing culture, well-being and strategy. HRM puts people as key resource for the company. 
### 2. What are the aims of HRM?
    Provide well-being to employees and align the enterprise culture with the strategy and objectives, looking forward to achieve overall goals. 

    Secondary goals: 
    - high performance culture
    - positive climate and trust
    - employees engaged
    - develop talents
### 3. How do globalization, demographic development and digitalization affect HRM?
    HRM are having new challenges, since hiring and keeping talents is becoming much more difficult. Globalization allows people to go work in other countries, which challenge HRMs to be ready to deal with cultural differences and to create a friendly environment for the newcomers.
