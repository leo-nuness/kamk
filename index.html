03. Landscape Analysis

Data Landscape: The Big Data Dimensions

Evaluated against the reference framework from Muniswamaiah et al. (2019).

VEL

High Velocity

Continuous real-time feeds from Digitraffic API streams and moving train IoT sensors. Requires streaming ingestion (Azure Event Hubs) rather than traditional batch runs.

Sources: #1, #4, #9
VAR

Format Variety

Spans structured JSON APIs (#1), geospatial map layers (#3), high-quality ticket records (#6), continuous time-series telemetry (#9), and unstructured text complaint emails (#8).

Demands Hybrid Storage
VER

Veracity Split

Operational timestamps and GPS coordinates have high veracity (ground truth). However, customer NPS surveys and complaints carry subjective and vocal-customer bias.

Objective vs. Subjective
VUL

Vulnerability & GDPR

Operational data is open, but commercial registers hold passenger PII. Compensation claims may contain GDPR Article 9 special-category health data (medical delays, injuries).

Strict EU Cloud Boundary
Architectural Implication: The sheer variety and speed necessitate a Cloud Data Lake for telemetry and text, paired with a Cloud Data Warehouse for ticketing transactions. Muniswamaiah et al. (2019)

Proposal A: Fleet Operations
Strategic Initiative 1

Weather-Aware Predictive Maintenance

Enriching internal bogie telemetry with external freezing weather and track-switch geodata.

01

Business Problem

Sub-zero temperatures (-30 °C), packed snow, and ice stress wheelsets and brakes. Unexpected trackside breakdowns cause cascading delays and emergency repairs costing up to 10× workshop servicing.

02

Data Required

Internal: Fleet sensor logs (#9: bogie vibration, temperature, pressure).
External: FMI weather forecasts (#2: frost, blizzards) + Väylävirasto geodata (#3: track switches, speed limits).

03

Cloud Services (PaaS)

Platform for live telemetry ingestion into an elastic Cloud Data Lake. Uses stream processing for immediate alarms and batch analytics for wear models.

04

Applied AI Models

Anomaly Detection flags abnormal vibration/heat. Predictive Analytics models correlate freezing ambient temperatures with component wear to forecast failure before it happens.

🔄 Next-Generation Cloud Upgrade: Builds directly on VR FleetCare's existing condition monitoring by adding environmental context. Problem → Data → Cloud → AI
Target Function: Fleet Management & Rolling Stock Operations Slide 05 / 13

Proposal B: Customer Care
Strategic Initiative 2

Smart Delay Management & Automated Claims

Tackling passenger disruption via cloud warehouse cross-referencing and the "Appropriate AI" split.

The Operational Challenge: Network-wide disruptions overwhelm support desks with manual refund claims and status questions (e.g., a single Midsummer incident affected ~4,000 passengers and generated ~€200,000 in claims).
RedBus Cloud Model
Tier 1: Machine Learning

Delay Propagation ML

Predictive algorithms analyze real-time Digitraffic feeds (#1) and weather alerts (#2) to forecast knock-on cumulative arrival delays across downstream stations, outperforming manual controller baselines.

Output: Dynamic Destination ETAs
Tier 2: Deterministic Rules (No AI)

Automated Standard Refunds

Because arrival delay is an objective truth in Digitraffic (#1), automated database business rules cross-check ticket records (#6) against verified arrival times. Standard delay compensation is approved instantly without AI overhead.

Output: Instant Refund Approvals
Tier 3: Generative AI & NLP

Complex Claims & Rerouting

Natural Language Processing categorizes free-text claims (#8) (hotel/taxi costs). GenAI drafts context-aware passenger disruption updates and suggests alternative regional bus/metro routes via Digitransit (#4).

Output: Draft Replies & Custom Alerts

Proposal B: Governance & Impact

Transforming the Disruption Customer Journey

Moving from slow, reactive backlogs to automated resolution with human empathy.

Service Process Division

⚡ Automated Fast Track (No Waiting) Seconds to Minutes

Standard delays (>60 min) automatically trigger push notification refunds. Disrupted travelers receive personalized alternative connection routes on their phones before arriving at the station.

🛡️ Human Support Agent Review (Empathy Gate) Protected Authority

Support staff review hotel expenses, taxi reimbursements, and any sensitive claims involving medical emergencies or passenger injuries. Managers authorize emergency bus contracts and mass broadcasts.

🔒 Ethics Guarantee: AI assists agents by drafting responses, but humans hold final approval over complex payouts and claim rejections.

05. Governance & Feasibility (1/2)

Risks & Feasibility: Privacy, Ethics & Trust

Rigorous compliance covering customer data and workplace decision-making.

GDPR

GDPR & Health Data

Risk: Ticket registers (#6) contain passenger contact info. Compensation claims (#8) may contain sensitive health data (missed medical appointments, injuries during travel).

Mitigation: Process all data strictly within EU cloud regions. Execute a full Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA). Apply automated PII redaction before LLM processing.

Mandatory DPIA & Strict RBAC
ETH

Workplace & Consumer Ethics

Risk: Algorithmic alerts could place undue pressure on depot mechanics or result in unfair automated rejections of complex customer refund claims.

Mitigation: Position AI strictly as assistive decision support. Prohibit algorithmic scoring of workshop/support staff. Require human sign-off on all non-standard claim rejections.

No Automated Claim Rejections
AI

AI Errors & Model Bias

Risk: IoT models risk false alarms during rare blizzards (unnecessary train withdrawals). Free-text GenAI risks hallucinating incorrect regional bus routes.

Mitigation: Ground GenAI strictly in verified live timetable APIs (RAG architecture). Enforce model confidence thresholds and require technician inspection before repairs.

API Grounding & Confidence Scores

05. Governance & Feasibility (2/2)

Risks & Feasibility: Cost, Quality & Multi-Cloud

Managing implementation budgets, arctic sensor reliability, and vendor portability.

€

Cost & Specialized Talent

Risk: Upfront IoT retrofitting on legacy trains and continuous streaming cloud compute fees can escalate; scarce MLOps and cloud engineering skills.

Mitigation: Adopt pay-as-you-go PaaS/AaaS tools with strict automated budget alarms. Launch a narrow corridor pilot on the Helsinki–Oulu line before nationwide scaling.

Phased Route Pilot Strategy
QLT

Data Quality & Heterogeneity

Risk: Sub-zero (-30 °C) arctic sensor dropouts (#9) and structural heterogeneity when joining different format databases.

Mitigation: Implement automated data cleaning at the ingestion layer to filter noisy sensor signals. Standardize all data models using shared keys: train number, station code, and UTC timestamp.

Unified Data Catalogue & Keys
PORT

Platform & Vendor Lock-In

Risk: Over-reliance on proprietary cloud AI APIs (Azure/AWS) and vulnerability to licensing changes in Fintraffic's open APIs.

Mitigation: Deploy models inside portable Docker/Kubernetes containers. Store analytical data in open table formats (Parquet) and maintain open REST API compatibility across clouds.

Containerized Multi-Cloud Architecture
